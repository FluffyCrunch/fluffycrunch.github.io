05About

Hi, I'm Shifa.
I make games as FluffyCrunch.

Education · 2021 — 2025

AISSMS College of Engineering

B.E. Computer Engineering

I started with a Phaser game about catching apples and kept going: a match-3 puzzler, a particle toy with its own GLSL shader, Pong you play with your bare hand, and a 3D tower defense game I liked enough to build in two engines.

I like the systems underneath too, like object pools, scene managers and wave data that two renderers can share. Right now I'm leveling up in Unity and C#, with Flappy Bird as the first build and bigger Unity projects on the way.

I also work full-stack (React, Node, Laravel, WebSockets), so the backend half of a game doesn't scare me.
