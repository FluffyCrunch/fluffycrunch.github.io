// 04 — QUEST LOG

Player Backstory

I'm Shifa, and I make games under the name FluffyCrunch. I started with a Phaser game about catching apples and kept going: a match-3 puzzler, a particle toy with its own GLSL shader, Pong you play with your bare hand, and a 3D tower defense game I liked enough to build in two engines.

What I care about most is game feel: the tween on a gem swap, screen shake that grows with a rally, a CPU opponent that's good but not perfect. I like the systems underneath too, like object pools, scene managers and wave data that two renderers can share.

Right now I'm leveling up in Unity and C#, with Flappy Bird as the first build and bigger Unity projects on the way. I also work full-stack (React, Node, Laravel, WebSockets), so the backend half of a game doesn't scare me.
